# 50 original board-skin plan

These are proposed original themes for our game, not Miniclip's inventory. The reference product page publicly names 12 arena labels, but that does not prove its full live board count.

All 50 skins must share identical board geometry, pocket dimensions, friction and collision tuning. Skins are visual only and must not affect shot outcomes. Do not trace or reuse Miniclip textures, logos, artwork or screenshots.

## Unlock and production rules

- Start with five free boards; unlock others through match milestones and achievements.
- Event access may be earned through clearly described free tasks.
- Shop boards must show a preview and fixed price.
- No board may change force, aim, friction, pocket size or collision response.
- Every theme needs a thumbnail, preview lighting, original texture set and asset-license manifest entry.
- Test physics equivalence on all 50 skins.
