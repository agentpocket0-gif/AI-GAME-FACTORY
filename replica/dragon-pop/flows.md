# Publicly evidenced flows

## F01 - Play a bubble-shot level

S02 Home (unknown) -> S03 Level selection (unknown) -> S04 Gameplay -> S05 Aim -> S06 Match/drop -> S04 next shot -> S07 Dragon rescue -> S09 Star result

Verified core: match at least three same-colour bubbles; remove bubbles to reveal hidden dragons; stars depend on performance.
Unverified: actual entry screens, shot queue, wall bounce, swap, fail condition, move limit, retry and next-level behavior.

## F02 - Use a prop

S04 Gameplay -> S08 Booster/prop action -> S04 Gameplay

The store listing confirms props exist and help pass levels. Names, charge counts, unlock source, ad reward, currency cost, cooldown and effects are unknown.

## F03 - Play offline

S02 Home -> S04 Gameplay -> background/close app -> resume

The store page advertises no-Wi-Fi play. Save point, resume behavior and offline availability of every mode have not been observed.

## F04 - Ad-supported play

Unknown trigger -> S11 Ad/reward flow -> return to game

Google Play confirms the app contains ads. No ad position, frequency cap, rewarded offer, interstitial trigger or ad-free purchase was verified. Our game must not interrupt an active shot.

## Evidence discipline

Only bubble-shoot, match, rescue, stars and offline play are strong enough to define core requirements now. Other routes remain provisional until a user-controlled walkthrough or authorized static package evidence is available.
